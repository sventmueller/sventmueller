## Professional work

**[Insurware](https://insurware.com)** provides digital insurance distribution and AI consulting for insurers
and financial services in Germany and Switzerland. Advice stays digital all the way to the contract:
brokers advise, customers sign, insurers receive complete applications, and every step is logged audit-proof.

- **Advisory Portal** for insurers, broker pools and sales teams: BaFin, IDD and FINMA compliant,
  and the IDD advice record is produced along the way, not afterwards
- **DataHub** for the back office: it reconciles the policies in broker management systems with insurer data
- **AVD Customer Journey** for end customers: they take out a pension savings plan themselves after a consultation
- **BiPRO AI Proxy** for broker pools and software vendors: it reads policies and endorsements from PDFs into BiPRO 430.4

**AI consulting:** applied AI in daily business with a human in the loop, model selection and operations,
software architecture and AI-assisted development, aligned with GDPR, the Swiss FADP, DORA and the EU AI Act.
Insurware itself is built with AI assistants, safeguarded by more than 100 automated quality gates.

My standard stack:

- **Backend:** Go and PostgreSQL with row-level security; sessions, queues and rate limits live in Postgres too
- **Frontend:** single-page apps with Vite, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and TanStack Router and Query
- **Operations:** a modern monolith, one binary, one database, one container, on Docker Compose

## Open source

100 % Rust with the native interface of each operating system, currently macOS on AppKit. No Electron.

- **[Splat](https://github.com/sventmueller/splat)**: an ultrafast, native Markdown editor for macOS.
  Write in the source or straight in the preview. Lines you did not touch stay byte-for-byte
  identical, so your Git diffs stay clean. A keystroke in a 10 MB document renders in about 10 ms.
  LaTeX, Mermaid, Graphviz and PlantUML work offline, it exports to PDF, HTML and Word, and it speaks 35 languages.
- **[Lowspeak](https://github.com/sventmueller/lowspeak)**: hold fn, talk, let go, and your words appear
  wherever the cursor is. Online text arrives about 0.4 s after you let go. Offline, NVIDIA Parakeet
  turns 40 seconds of speech into text in about a third of a second. 35 languages.
